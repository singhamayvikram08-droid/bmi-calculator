Privacy & Data Sovereignty Policy

VitalAI Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 13, 2026  |  Version: 2.4.0

1. Local-First Architecture

VitalAI is engineered around a strict local-first paradigm. All core mathematical evaluations—including Body Mass Index (BMI), clinical body fat estimation (Deurenberg clinical formula), basal metabolic calculations (Mifflin-St Jeor), and fitness vitality index scores—are computed directly on your client device.

Your biological parameters (height, weight, age, and biological gender) are never uploaded to any centralized advertising database or shared with data brokers.

2. Local Storage & Client Persistence

To provide tracking charts across visits without requiring invasive backend accounts, VitalAI uses browser localStorage to store:

  • User display name and authenticated email (demo simulation session)
  • Biometric calculation history (date, BMI score, vitality rating)
  • Theme preferences (dark/light mode) and measurement unit toggles (metric/imperial)
  • Optional user-provided OpenRouter API access credentials

You retain complete sovereignty over this data. Clicking Reset History or clearing your browser cookies permanently purges all stored metrics instantly.

3. Ephemeral AI Consultations

When you request a personalized AI nutrition or fitness plan, requests are sent securely to OpenRouter or authorized model backends. Transmitted prompts contain only the anonymous parameters needed for wellness generation (age, gender, BMI range, and calorie targets). These requests are processed ephemerally and are never retained for machine learning model training.

4. Zero Commercial Tracking Cookies

VitalAI does not deploy cross-site tracking cookies, behavioral tracking pixels, or third-party advertising trackers. We employ privacy-first, aggregate metrics solely to monitor platform health, uptime, and UI error rates.

5. Transport Security & HTTPS

All traffic is strictly encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS). Insecure connections are systematically upgraded via Content Security Policies.

6. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Under global privacy frameworks including GDPR and CCPA, you have the right to access, rectify, export, and delete your data at any time. Because your data resides within your browser client, you may exercise complete deletion instantly using the in-app controls or by purging browser site data.
